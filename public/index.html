HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS

Your road to all 99s.

Hours left = Σ (XP left in band ÷ band rate)

EHP and time to max use 2004Tracker’s level-based skill rates. The scan’s top-five records are a separate comparison.

The target is 13,034,431 XP per skill. Training rates change at the source’s level boundaries. XP beyond 99 adds EHP at the final rate and no remaining time.

Calculation & coverage details

Remaining time sums the XP in each remaining level band divided by that band’s rate. EHP does the same for earned XP. The fastest listed method is used where bands overlap. Cooking levels 35–40 carry forward the preceding rate because the source has a gap.

Hitpoints has no source rate and adds no separate EHP or training time; the estimate assumes it is trained alongside combat. All 19 skills still count toward the XP target and 99 count. Bonus XP deductions, supplies and banking are excluded; this is a sum of training requirements, not an optimized elapsed-time plan.

The local copy of the linked rate file is independent of scan progress and the activity window. The window only affects reported personal averages and the inspected player sample. The leaderboard is limited to inspected characters.

Scanned records use full one-hour windows with chart rounding tolerance. Gaps over 30 minutes and XP resets are excluded. Each player contributes one best observed hour per skill.
